About the Event

The Journey Begins

INNOVISTA is a journey from observation and discovery to innovation and design, encouraging participants to identify hidden problems within local ecosystems and create meaningful solutions. Drawing inspiration from Leonardo da Vinci's insatiable curiosity and Renaissance ingenuity, this competition challenges teams to observe nature's subtle disruptions, hypothesize solutions, and build prototypes that bridge the gap between scientific understanding and real-world impact.

"The noblest pleasure is the joy of understanding." — Leonardo da Vinci

Eco-Theme

The Hidden Glitches

Six ecological challenges hiding in plain sight — each one a call for innovation.

Pollution

Air, water, and soil contamination disrupting natural cycles.

"Observe the invisible"

Water Wastage

Inefficient usage and mismanagement of freshwater resources.

"Every drop holds knowledge"

Urban Heat

Rising temperatures from concrete landscapes and deforestation.

"Nature's thermometer"

Plastic Accumulation

Persistent plastic waste choking ecosystems and food chains.

"The material that lingers"

Energy Inefficiency

Excessive consumption and reliance on non-renewable sources.

"Power unmeasured"

Biodiversity Imbalance

Disrupted ecosystems threatening species and natural harmony.

"Life's delicate web"
Competition Structure

The Rounds

I
Day 1 — July 25

ENIGMA

Round 1 The Discovery Quest

A treasure hunt across the campus where teams decode clues, solve puzzles, and uncover insights about local ecosystem glitches. Each clue reveals a hidden piece of the larger environmental puzzle.

Rules

  • Teams of 3-4 members
  • Follow clue sequence strictly
  • No external assistance
  • Complete within time limit
  • Document findings in sketchbook

Judging Criteria

  • Accuracy of Clues Solved
  • Time Management
  • Teamwork & Coordination
  • Problem-Solving Approach
  • Rule Adherence & Fair Play
Clue stations hidden across 5 zones
Round 2 The Innovation Lab

A hands-on workshop where teams translate their discoveries into blueprints. Using provided materials and scientific principles, participants design solutions and build working models.

Workshop Structure

  • Problem analysis session
  • Blueprint & sketch development
  • Prototype model building
  • Scientific documentation

Judging Criteria

  • Active Participation
  • Understanding of Concepts
  • Creativity & Idea Development
  • Application of Learning
  • Team Interaction & Collaboration
II
Day 2 — July 26

AAVISHKAR

Final Round The Solution Arena

Teams present their completed prototypes before a panel of judges. This is where discovery transforms into design — showcasing working models, scientific reasoning, and the potential for real-world impact.

Presentation Format

  • Working prototype demonstration
  • Scientific explanation (5 min)
  • Judges' Q&A (3 min)
  • Poster & documentation display

Judging Criteria

  • Innovation & Originality
  • Practical Applicability
  • Science Behind the Solution
  • Feasibility of Prototype
  • Clarity & Confidence
  • Problem Identification & Understanding
  • Model Design & Working
