CBA

An advanced engineering and design sprint where teams build prototypes to solve real-world sustainability challenges under time constraints.

Event Overview

CBA is an intense design sprint that challenges participants to think on their feet. Teams are presented with a pressing sustainability crisis and must rapidly ideate, prototype, and pitch a viable solution — all within a limited timeframe.

From green energy to waste management, the scenarios are drawn from real-world issues. This event emphasizes practical engineering, feasibility, and the ability to communicate complex ideas under pressure.

Rules

  • Teams of 3-4 members
  • 2.5 hours design sprint
  • Prototype or blueprint required
  • Top teams proceed to final round

Judging Criteria

  • Feasibility & Practicality
  • Innovation & Design
  • Presentation & Pitch
  • Team Collaboration
